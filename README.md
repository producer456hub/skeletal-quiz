# 🦴 Bone & Skeletal System — Histology Quiz

An interactive, browser-based study quiz covering the **skeletal system & bone tissue** (Anatomy & Physiology, Weeks 7–9).

**▶ Play it:** https://producer456hub.github.io/skeletal-quiz/

## What's inside
- **150 multiple-choice questions** across three parts:
  - **Part 1** — bone anatomy, growth (appositional vs. interstitial), the epiphyseal plate, bone nutrients & calcium homeostasis
  - **Part 2** — cell lineages, intramembranous & endochondral ossification, fracture repair, PTH/calcitonin regulation
  - **Part 3** — bone matrix, compact/spongy histology, fracture types & joint classification
- **Slide identification round** — identify real virtual-microscope slides and the **cellular makeup of bone** (osteocytes in lacunae, osteoblasts lining trabeculae, multinucleated osteoclasts in resorption pits, canaliculi, osteons & lamellae). Each slide links to the full zoomable viewer.

## Features
- Pick a mode (full quiz, individual part, slide round, or everything)
- Optional question shuffle & instant feedback
- Progress bar, live score, animated results ring, and full answer review
- Keyboard shortcuts: press **1–5** or **A–E** to answer, **Enter** for next
